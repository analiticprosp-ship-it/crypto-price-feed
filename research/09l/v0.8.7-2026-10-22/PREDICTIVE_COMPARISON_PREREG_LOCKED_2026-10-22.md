# 09L v0.8.7 — FROZEN model comparison design, 2026-10-22 UTC
**Commit before baseline code, evaluation, or reading holdout results. SHADOW / RESEARCH_ONLY.**

## Research question and safeguards
Does adding *pre-anchor 30-second taker-flow imbalance* improve the **directional prediction of future BTC price** over a purely price/trade-count baseline? This is **not** proof of a liquidation cascade or CoinGlass-cluster attraction. All source event clocks and the 9 studies were fixed and prepared before this comparison, but historic labels have already been inspected in earlier research: call this a **retrospective time-split diagnostic, not a prospectively sealed OOS test**.

## Dataset integrity
Frozen sources exactly nine derived `research/09l/auto/v1/derived/v087_labels/{venue}/{date}_no_lookahead_trade_labels.json` studies: Bybit BTCUSDT 2026-06-01, 07-01, 08-01, 09-01, 10-01; OKX BTC-USDT-SWAP 2026-06-01,07-01,08-01,10-01. Source status must be OK; `trading_canon_unchanged == true`; timestamps/source SHA; all features marked `past_features_only` and strictly pre-anchor. Eight pre-fixed times per day. No raw raw-volume aggregation across venues (use separately normalized ratios).
- **Training**: 2026-06-01, 07-01 and 08-01, all eligible venue × eight-clock observations. **Holdout**: 2026-09-01 and 10-01; no fitting on the holdout or dynamic choice of thresholds/features/lambda. Date grouping is the primary unit; Bybit+OKX on the same day/time share the common BTC price path, not independent markets.
- **Primary target** +300 second signed return >0 versus <0. Exactly 0 return -> `FLAT_TARGET_EXCLUDED`. As-of >2s/missing -> excluded per outcome independently. Secondary +30s and +60s using same settings. Do not substitute stale returns, do not choose successful horizons posthoc.
- Prediction time is strictly **before t0**. Feature 1 past 30s momentum `past_30s_price_return_bps`; feature 2 `past_30s_high_low_range_bps`; feature 3 `log1p(n_trades_past_30s)`; feature 4 venue dummy `okex-swap=1, bybit=0`. Augmented adds only `raw_flow_imbalance` of aggressor buy/sell amount from *past [-30s,t0)*. Cases with any missing non-finite feature excluded and counted, never imputed. The as-of current reference price cannot be used as future predictive feature if it is later than t0; source captures are <=t0.
- Freeze **three models**: constant training prevalence with Laplace add-one smoothing; deterministic ridge logistic baseline (features1..4), deterministic ridge logistic augmented (features1..5); L2 lambda=2.0 applied to all coefficients except intercept. Standardization uses TRAIN mean/variance only, scale floor 1e-9, clipping standardized values [-5,5] both train and heldout. Solve Newton/IRLS, max 60 iterations with stable sigmoid and convergence norm <1e-9. No learned hyperparameter search, feature selection, non-linearity, posthoc thresholds.
- Primary diagnostic: 300s Brier score (mean (p-y)^2) and Bernoulli logloss; accuracy with fixed 0.5 threshold secondary, per calendar date and per venue. Report *sample-weighted* and **calendar-day equal-weighted** Brier; for duplicate venues at an event clock, compare paired bps or loss within month rather than treating them as independent.
- Accept a promising signal for *further prospective testing* only if baseline→augmented day-equal Brier improves on **both** holdout dates, paired logloss also improves and calibration is not worse in each venue; otherwise `NO_REPLICATED_INCREMENTAL_GAIN`. This is merely a cautious exploration gate; **never** approve a trading signal based on these two dates. Even if it passes, `UNVALIDATED_REQUIRES_PROSPECTIVE_PIT`.
- Do not calculate confidence intervals or p-values over 62 venue/windows as independent Bernoulli trials. Report 9 cohort files, missingness, and effective **5 calendar dates**, 2 holdout dates. **CoinGlass Model2 PIT remains missing; signed dealer gamma unknown.**
